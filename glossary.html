Glossary

Vocabulary and notation, grouped by week

Symbols follow the lecture slides. Where a week’s page uses a symbol, it explains it there too.

Week 1 · Introduction to Robot Learning

Symbol or term Meaning
\(s_t\), \(o_t\), \(a_t\) State of the world, the robot’s observation of it, and its action, at time step \(t\)
\(\hat{s}_t\) The robot’s estimate of the state, from its sensors
\(\pi\) Policy: the rule that picks an action from what the robot observes
\(f_\theta\), \(\ell\) A model with parameters \(\theta\), and the loss it is trained to minimize
i.i.d. Independent and identically distributed data, the usual assumption of supervised learning
\(B\), \(\Delta\) Number of bins per action dimension, and the bin width
\(k\) An action token: the index of a bin, from 0 to \(B-1\)
\(\hat{a}\) The bin centre a token stands for, the action the robot executes
Sense-plan-act Perceive once, plan a complete path, execute it without looking again
Open loop, closed loop Executing a fixed plan, versus choosing each action from the latest observation
Vision-language-action model (VLA) A vision-language model fine-tuned to output robot actions as tokens

Week 2 · Robot Control & MDPs

Symbol or term Meaning
SE(2), SE(3) Rigid-body poses in the plane (3 numbers) and in space (6 numbers)
\(R\), \(\mathbf{t}\), \(T\) A rotation matrix, a translation vector, and the homogeneous transform built from them
\(q\) Joint configuration: one angle (or length) per joint
\(x = f(q)\) Forward kinematics: the end-effector pose for joint configuration \(q\)
\(J(q)\) Jacobian: how the end-effector moves for a small change of each joint
C-space Configuration space: the set of all joint configurations
Workspace, task space Points the end effector can reach, and the space the task is defined in
\(f(s)\), \(s \in [0, 1]\) Time scaling of a move: \(s\) is the share of the move’s time used so far
\(e_k\), \(u_k\) Tracking error and motor command at control step \(k\)
\(K_p\), \(K_i\), \(K_d\) Proportional, integral and derivative gains of a PID controller
\(\pi_\theta(a_t \mid o_t)\) Policy with parameters \(\theta\): a distribution over actions given the observation
\(\mathcal{M} = (\mathcal{S}, \mathcal{A}, P, R)\) A Markov decision process: states, actions, transition probabilities, reward function
\(P(s_{t+1} \mid s_t, a_t)\) Transition probability (dynamics); ADM writes \(T(s' \mid s, a)\)
\(r_t\), \(\gamma\) Reward at step \(t\), and the discount factor in \([0, 1)\)
\(\tau\), \(p_\pi(\tau)\) A trajectory \((s_0, a_0, s_1, \dots)\) and its probability under policy \(\pi\)
\(G\), \(J(\pi)\) Return of one trajectory, and its expected value under \(\pi\)
\(\delta\), \(\nu\) A random search direction, and the size of the nudge along it
Markov property The next state depends only on the current state and action, not on earlier ones
Sparse, dense reward Reward only on success, versus a reward that measures progress at every step

Week 3 · Imitation Learning

Symbol or term Meaning
\(s_t\), \(o_t\), \(a_t\) State, observation and action at time step \(t\)
\(\pi^*\) The expert policy being imitated (ADM writes \(\pi_E\))
\(\pi_\theta\) The learned policy, a network with parameters \(\theta\)
\(\mathcal{D}\) Demonstration data set of (state, expert action) pairs
\(c(s, a)\) Cost of a step: 0 if \(a\) matches the expert’s action, 1 otherwise
\(\varepsilon\) Probability that the learned policy makes a mistake on a state the expert visits
\(T\) Task length (horizon) in steps
\(p_{\text{expert}}(s)\), \(p_{\pi_\theta}(s)\) How often the expert, or the learned policy, visits state \(s\)
\(w_k, \mu_k, \Sigma_k\) Weight, mean and covariance of component \(k\) of a mixture-of-Gaussians policy
\(a_{t,d}\) Dimension \(d\) of the action, predicted as a bin in an autoregressive policy
\(z\) Latent plan: a random input that selects one way of doing the task
\(s_c\), \(s_g\) Current state and goal state in a goal-conditioned policy
\(q_\phi\), \(p(z \mid c)\) Posterior (“plan recognition”) and prior (“plan proposal”) of a latent-variable policy
Behavioral cloning (BC) Supervised learning of a policy from expert (state, action) pairs
DAgger Dataset Aggregation: label the learner’s own states with expert actions, add them, retrain
Distribution shift Test-time states differ from the states in the training data
Causal confusion A policy relies on a correlate of the expert’s action instead of its cause

Week 4 · Reinforcement Learning I

Symbol or term Meaning
\(V^\pi(s)\) Value of state \(s\): expected discounted return when starting there and following \(\pi\) (ADM writes \(U^\pi\))
\(V^*(s)\), \(\pi^*\) Optimal value function, and a policy that achieves it
\(Q^\pi(s, a)\), \(Q^*(s, a)\) Action value: expected return after taking \(a\) in \(s\), then following \(\pi\) (or acting optimally)
\(\mathcal{T}\) Bellman (optimality) operator: one sweep of value iteration
TD target \(r + \gamma \max_{a'} Q(s', a')\) (Q-learning) or \(r + \gamma Q(s', a')\) (SARSA): a one-sample estimate of \(Q(s, a)\)
\(\alpha\) Learning rate of a tabular update
\(\varepsilon\)-greedy Act randomly with probability \(\varepsilon\), otherwise take the best action
On-policy, off-policy Learning the value of the policy that collects the data, versus of another (usually the greedy) policy
\(\theta\), \(\theta^-\) Weights of the Q-network and of its slowly updated target copy
\(\mathcal{D}\) Replay buffer of stored transitions \((s, a, r, s')\)
\(\mu_\phi(s)\) DDPG’s actor: a network that outputs the action directly
CEM Cross-entropy method: sample, keep the best, refit a Gaussian, repeat

Week 5 · Reinforcement Learning II

Symbol or term Meaning
\(\pi_\theta(a \mid s)\) A policy with parameters \(\theta\), here a distribution over actions
\(\tau\), \(p_\theta(\tau)\) A trajectory \((s_0, a_0, s_1, \dots)\) and its probability under \(\pi_\theta\) and the dynamics
\(R(\tau)\), \(J(\theta)\) A trajectory’s return, and the expected return of the policy (ADM writes \(U(\theta)\))
\(\nabla_\theta \log \pi_\theta(a \mid s)\) Score function: the direction that makes action \(a\) more likely
Reward-to-go \(\sum_{t' \ge t} r_{t'}\): the rewards from step \(t\) to the end
\(b\) Baseline subtracted from the return; may depend on the state, never on the action
\(A^\pi(s, a)\) Advantage \(Q^\pi(s, a) - V^\pi(s)\): how much better \(a\) is than the policy’s average
\(\theta\), \(\theta'\) The policy that collected the data, and the new policy being optimized
\(\rho\) Importance ratio \(\pi_{\theta'}(a \mid s) / \pi_\theta(a \mid s)\)
\(\mathcal{H}(\pi)\) Entropy of the policy’s action distribution
\(D_{\text{KL}}\) KL divergence between two distributions
\(\epsilon\) PPO’s clip range: ratios are clipped to \([1 - \epsilon, 1 + \epsilon]\)
\(\beta\), \(\alpha\) Entropy temperature in the lecture’s entropy bonus (\(\beta\)) and in SAC (\(\alpha\))
\(\delta\) TRPO’s trust-region size, a bound on the average KL divergence
\(Q_\phi\), \(Q_{\bar\phi}\) SAC’s critic and its slowly updated target copy
\(\mu_\theta(s)\), \(\sigma_\theta(s)\), \(\varepsilon\) Mean, standard deviation and fixed noise of a reparameterized action \(a = \mu + \sigma\varepsilon\)

Week 6 · Generative Models

Symbol or term Meaning
\(x_0\) A clean data point: an action, an action sequence, or an image
\(x_i\), \(i = 1, \dots, T\) The same point after \(i\) noising steps; \(T\) steps in all
\(\beta_i\), \(\alpha_i = 1 - \beta_i\) Noise schedule: how much noise step \(i\) adds, and how much signal it keeps
\(\bar\alpha_i\) Signal kept after \(i\) steps: the product of \(\alpha_1, \dots, \alpha_i\)
\(\varepsilon\), \(\varepsilon_\theta(x_i, i)\) Standard Gaussian noise, and the network’s prediction of it
\(z\), \(p(z)\) A latent code and its prior, usually \(\mathcal{N}(0, I)\)
\(q_\phi(z \mid x)\), \(p_\theta(x \mid z)\) Encoder and decoder of a VAE
\(e_k\), \(\mathrm{sg}[\cdot]\) Codebook vector \(k\) of a VQ-VAE, and the stop-gradient operator
\(w\), \(c\), \(\varnothing\) Guidance scale, condition, and the “no condition” token
\(t \in [0, 1]\), \(v_\theta(x_t, t)\) Flow-matching time (noise at 0, data at 1) and the learned velocity
DDPM, DDIM Diffusion sampling with fresh noise at every step, and deterministic sampling with fewer steps

Week 7 · Sequence Modeling and Transformers

Symbol or term Meaning
\(x_{1:i-1}\), \(x_{<t}\) The prefix of a sequence: every element before position \(i\) (or \(t\))
\(p_\theta(x_i \mid x_{1:i-1})\) An autoregressive model’s conditional: the next element given all earlier ones
\(h_t\) Hidden state of a recurrent network after step \(t\)
\(Q, K, V\); \(q_i, k_j, v_j\) Queries, keys and values (matrices, and one token’s vectors)
\(W_Q, W_K, W_V\) Learned projections from token embeddings to queries, keys and values
\(d\) Width of the query and key vectors; scores are divided by \(\sqrt{d}\)
\(s_{ij}\), \(\alpha_{ij}\) Attention score of token \(i\) for token \(j\), and its softmax weight
\(\mathrm{PE}(i)\), \(b(i - j)\) Absolute positional encoding of position \(i\); relative bias for distance \(i - j\)
\(\lambda\) Slope of the goal toy’s relative bias, \(b(i - j) = -\lambda (i - j)\)
Teacher forcing Training on the true previous tokens instead of the model’s own outputs
BPE Byte-pair encoding: merge the most frequent adjacent pair, \(k\) times
\(N\), \(D\), \(C\) Parameters, training tokens and training compute (\(C \approx 6ND\))
\(s_{ij}\), \(\tau\) (CLIP) Image-caption cosine similarity and the softmax temperature
\(\alpha\) (Flamingo) A layer’s gate: cross-attention output is scaled by \(\tanh(\alpha)\), with \(\alpha = 0\) at the start
\(q_{01}\), \(q_{99}\) 1st and 99th percentiles of an action dimension, used to normalize before binning
\(k\), \(a_{t:t+k}\) Chunk length, and the chunk of actions a policy predicts at once
\(A_t[i]\), \(w_i\), \(m\) Predictions stored for step \(t\) (\(i = 0\) the oldest), their weights \(e^{-mi}\), and the decay rate
\(c_j\), \(q_j\) DCT coefficient for frequency \(j\), and its rounded, scaled value

Week 8 · World Models

Symbol or term Meaning
\(p(s_{t+1} \mid s_t, a_t, h_t)\) A world model: the next state given state, action and memory
\(\pi(a_t \mid s_t, g)\) A goal-conditioned policy, for contrast
\(\hat s_{t+k}\) A state predicted (imagined) \(k\) steps ahead by the model
\(H\) Planning or imagination horizon, in steps
\(c(\hat s)\) Cost of an imagined state in model predictive control
\(\varepsilon\), \(L\), \(e_k\) One-step model error, how much the dynamics stretch an error, and the error after \(k\) imagined steps
\(o_t\), \(z_t\), \(h_t\) Observation, its latent code, and the recurrent memory
\(q(z_t \mid h_t, o_t)\), \(p(z_t \mid h_t)\) RSSM posterior (sees the observation) and prior (memory only)
\(\pi_i, \mu_i, \sigma_i\) Weight, mean and spread of a mixture component in World Models’ memory network
\(v_\psi\) Dreamer’s critic, a value estimate of imagined states
\(c_s\), \(c_t\) A video tokenizer’s compression factor in each spatial direction and in time
\(\ell\), \(g_\psi\) Language instruction; inverse dynamics model that maps observations to actions
\(\mathrm{sg}[\cdot]\), \(\bar\theta\) Stop-gradient, and the target encoder’s weights (an EMA or a frozen copy)
MPC, CEM Model predictive control; the cross-entropy method used to search plans
WAM, VAM, IDM World action model; video action model; inverse dynamics model
JEPA Joint-embedding predictive architecture: predicts embeddings, not pixels

Week 9 · Generalist Robot Policies

Symbol or term Meaning
\(g\) A goal: a goal image or a language instruction \(\ell\)
\(e\), \(d_e\), \(D\) An embodiment, its number of action dimensions, and the largest \(d_e\) (the padded size)
\(m_{ij}\) Loss mask: 1 for a real action entry, 0 for padding or another robot’s head
\(f_k\) Multicontext imitation: the encoder of task descriptions of kind \(k\) into a shared goal space
\(m(b, l)\) Gato’s loss mask: 1 if token \(l\) of sequence \(b\) is text or an action
\(\mu\), \(M\) Mu-law parameters (Gato: \(\mu = 100\), \(M = 256\))
\(L_i\), \(B\) Token length of sample \(i\); samples in a batch
\(c_i\) (cu_seqlens) Where sample \(i\) starts in a packed sequence: the sum of the lengths before it
\(\mathrm{sg}[\cdot]\) Stop-gradient: the action expert reads the backbone but sends it no gradient
\(A\), \(I_t\), \(\epsilon_\ell\) Advantage, the improvement indicator \(\mathbb{1}[A > \epsilon_\ell]\), and its task threshold (π*0.6)
\(R_i\), \(R_{S,i}\) Real and simulated success rate of policy \(i\)
MMRV Mean maximum rank violation: how badly a simulator misorders policies, weighted by their real gap
SysID System identification: fitting a simulator’s parameters to measurements of the real robot
VLA Vision-language-action model: a VLM fine-tuned to output actions
OXE Open X-Embodiment, the pooled data set of over a million real-robot episodes

Week 10 · Embodied Reasoning and Test-time Scaling

Symbol or term Meaning
\(c\) The reasoning chain written before the action (plan, subtask, move, objects, gripper)
\(p_{\text{drop}}\), \(m\) Reasoning-dropout probability, and the mask that hides the reasoning (\(m = 0\))
ECoT, ECoT-Lite Embodied chain-of-thought; its recipes that write no reasoning at test time
\(N\) Number of samples drawn at test time (best-of-N)
\(p\) Chance that one sample (or one answer) is right
\(V(o, a)\) A verifier’s score for candidate \(a\)
\(\sigma_V\), \(w\) The toy verifier’s error on the gap position; the gap’s width
\(E_A\), \(\Delta\) An Elo rating; a rating gap
\(L\), \(T\) Transformer layers; reasoning tokens written before the answer
\(q\), \(o_i\), \(G\) A question, the \(i\)-th sampled answer, and the group size (GRPO)
\(r_i\), \(\hat A_{i,t}\) Answer \(i\)’s reward, and its group-relative advantage, shared by all its tokens \(t\)
\(\rho_{i,t}\) Probability ratio of token \(t\) of answer \(i\), new policy over old
\(\beta\), \(\pi_{\text{ref}}\) KL weight and the frozen reference policy
GRPO Group relative policy optimization: PPO with a group-average baseline instead of a critic
Verifiable reward A reward a rule can compute, such as checking the final answer

Week 11 · Frontiers & Open Problems

Symbol or term Meaning
\(N(s, a)\) How often the data take action \(a\) in state \(s\)
\(\mathcal{D}\), \(\tau\) A data set of trajectories; one trajectory
\(g = s_{t'}\) A goal relabelled in hindsight: a state the trajectory reached later
\(V(s)\) Cost-to-go (steps to the goal) through transitions in the data
\(N_e\), \(M\), \(k\) Expert episodes, autonomous episodes per round, rounds of the flywheel
\(\pi_m\), \(E\), \(u(o)\) Ensemble member \(m\) of \(E\); the members’ disagreement on observation \(o\)
\(\tau\) (threshold) Disagreement above which the robot asks for help
Coverage, selective success Share of tasks the robot takes on; its success on those
\(x_i\), \(y_i\) Normalized embeddings of image \(i\) and caption \(i\)
\(t\), \(b\) Contrastive temperature; SigLIP’s bias
\(z_{ij}\) SigLIP’s label: \(+1\) for a matching image-caption pair, \(-1\) otherwise
\(f\) Fraction of the data with a property (a possible hard negative)
Stitching Joining pieces of different trajectories into a new route
Data flywheel Deploy, collect, retrain, deploy a better robot
Mid-training Training after pre-training that adds core skills before fine-tuning

Overloaded symbols

Symbols that mean different things in different weeks.

Symbol Meaning Week

\(\alpha\) Step size of a gradient or random-search update 2
\(\alpha\) Learning rate of a tabular TD update 4
\(\varepsilon\) Exploration probability of an \(\varepsilon\)-greedy policy 4
\(\mu\) Mean of a Gaussian policy, or DDPG’s deterministic actor \(\mu_\phi(s)\) 4
\(T\) A homogeneous transform (a \(3 \times 3\) or \(4 \times 4\) matrix) 2
\(T\) Task length (horizon) in steps 3
\(T\) Number of diffusion steps 6
\(f\) Forward kinematics \(f(q)\), or a time scaling \(f(s)\); also a control frequency in Hz 2
\(J\) The Jacobian \(J(q)\), or the expected return \(J(\pi)\) 2
\(J(\theta)\) Expected return as a function of the policy’s parameters 5
\(\epsilon\) PPO’s clip range 5
\(\varepsilon\) Fixed Gaussian noise of a reparameterized action 5
\(\alpha\) SAC’s entropy temperature 5
\(\beta\) Entropy temperature of the lecture’s entropy bonus 5
\(\beta_i\) Noise added by diffusion step \(i\) 6
\(\delta\) TRPO’s trust-region size; on this page also the mean shift between two Gaussians, in standard deviations 5
\(\alpha_{ij}\) Attention weight of token \(i\) on token \(j\) 7
\(\alpha\) Flamingo’s cross-attention gate, used as \(\tanh(\alpha)\) 7
\(\tau\) CLIP’s softmax temperature (elsewhere a trajectory) 7
\(k\) Action-chunk length; also a number of BPE merges, and \(k_j\) a key vector 7
\(N\), \(D\) Parameters and training tokens in scaling laws; also \(N\) samples per chunk in the DCT 7
\(q_j\), \(q_i\) A rounded DCT coefficient, or a query vector 7
\(\pi_i\) A mixture weight in World Models’ memory network (not a policy) 8
\(H\) Planning horizon in steps; also an image’s height in the video-token count 8
\(L\) How much one step of the dynamics can stretch an error 8
\(\varepsilon\) One-step error of a world model 8
\(q\) RSSM posterior over the latent, \(q(z_t \mid h_t, o_t)\) 8
\(\mu\) Mu-law compression parameter in Gato’s tokenizer (elsewhere a mean) 9
\(M\) Mu-law range parameter; \(m\) is a loss mask 9
\(I_t\) π*0.6’s improvement indicator, 1 when the advantage beats the threshold (not an identity matrix) 9
\(\epsilon_\ell\) π*0.6’s advantage threshold for task \(\ell\) 9
\(o_i\) The \(i\)-th sampled answer in a GRPO group (not an observation) 10
\(c\) A reasoning chain (elsewhere a constant) 10
\(\beta\) Weight of GRPO’s KL penalty to the reference policy 10
\(N\) Samples drawn by best-of-N search 10
\(L\) Number of transformer layers 10
\(T\) Reasoning tokens written before the answer 10
\(\tau\) A disagreement threshold (elsewhere a trajectory) 11
\(t\) Contrastive temperature (elsewhere a time step) 11
\(M\) Autonomous episodes per round of the flywheel 11
\(V(s)\) Steps to the goal (a cost, minimized), not a reward-based value 11
